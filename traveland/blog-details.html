How to plan a trip that feels like you

Blog / Travel tips

14 June 2023 · Travel tips

The best itineraries start with a feeling, not a list of landmarks. Before you choose flights or hotels, think about how you want the trip to feel: energising, restorative, curious or full of food.

Build around the moments

Choose two or three experiences that matter most and give them space. A little unplanned time often becomes the most memorable part of a journey.

Leave room to change your mind

Good planning gives you confidence without taking away spontaneity. We recommend a clear framework, flexible bookings and local advice when you arrive.
